# RABT visual identity

Brand idea: linked forms connecting people and technology. The SVG mark was drawn in code; interface icons use the existing Lucide library.

Palette: olive `#4b6a37`, deep olive `#25392b`, glow `#c3ef74`, ivory `#f6f7f2`. Dark background `#111710`. Arabic-first RTL, restrained glow and reduced-motion support.

## Generated assets

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Each result was inspected, copied into the workspace, and optimized to WebP using Sharp. Original PNGs remain in `public/images/*-source.png`.

- `public/images/hero.webp`: homepage editorial photograph.
- `public/images/phone.webp`: illustrative phone category photograph.
- `public/images/laptop.webp`: illustrative laptop category photograph.
- `public/images/headphones.webp`: illustrative audio category photograph.
- `public/brand-mark.svg` and `src/app/icon.svg`: vector logo and site icon.

These are illustrative category photographs, reused by category and labeled on the website. They do not document the exact named models or actual inventory. Replace them with real seller photographs for commercial listings.

## Final generation prompts

### Hero

Use case: product-mockup. Create one premium 1536x1024 landscape editorial product photograph for a Jordanian technology marketplace called RABT. No text or logos. A sculptural still life of a dark olive modern laptop, black wireless over-ear headphones, a silver smartphone seen from the back with two cameras, and an ivory mechanical keyboard arranged on staggered deep olive stone display plinths. Warm ivory studio backdrop transitioning into muted olive on the left, soft natural window light, subtle lime green rim light, rich realistic materials, high-end commercial photography. Composition: objects grouped center-left with generous breathing room, all objects fully visible, highly polished, authentic photographic detail. Save image for website hero and product editorial imagery; output the image.
